Bone Formation and Remodeling

This project was a science presentation about how bones form, remodel themselves, maintain homeostasis, and repair themselves after an injury. I researched topics including calcium regulation, hormones involved in bone maintenance, and the stages that happen when a broken bone heals.

NVIDIA + Apple Investment Proposal

This project was an investment proposal focused on NVIDIA and Apple. I researched both companies and looked at information such as financial performance, business risks, technology trends, market conditions, and factors that could affect the companies in the future.

I used the research to create an investment proposal and explain why the two companies could work together as part of an investment portfolio. This helped me practice financial research, comparing companies, working with information, and creating a presentation.
